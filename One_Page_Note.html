1. Tools & Technologies Used

This solution implements an automated, zero-latency lead ingestion and response architecture using industry-standard cloud and AI tools:

  • Intake Layer: Google Forms – captures prospect name, email, phone/WhatsApp, domain interest, and project requirements.
  • Central CRM & State Logging: Google Sheets – acts as the live database for real-time lead tracking, timestamps, and status flags.
  • Execution & Orchestration: Google Apps Script / Webhooks – event-driven serverless pipeline executing on form submission.
  • AI Reasoning Engine: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash API – contextual prompt engineering generating bespoke, human-sounding replies.
  • Omnichannel Dispatch: Gmail API / WhatsApp Webhooks – delivers instant personalized emails and customer confirmations.

2. How the Automation Works (Architecture)

The pipeline operates on a four-stage asynchronous workflow:

  • Trigger & Ingestion: Prospect submits the Google Form. An event listener (onFormSubmit) immediately captures the payload.
  • AI Prompt Contextualization: The engine formats client data and prompt parameters, instructing the LLM to directly address the specific query and propose actionable next steps rather than sending canned generic replies.
  • CRM State Update: The AI response, timestamp, and status (Processed & Dispatched) are written back to Google Sheets.
  • Customer Dispatch: The lead instantly receives a tailored email from the PPS Anantams AI desk with formal inquiry confirmation.
